# Requirements

Full specification: [SRS v2 (Word)](files/GoLocalWheels_SRS_v2.docx). This page is the short version.

## Goal
Help tourists in India rent bikes, scooters and cycles from **local** rental shops, starting with Pondicherry. Give small shops an online presence they do not have today.

## Users
| User | Needs |
|---|---|
| Tourist / student | Find nearby shops, compare prices, book, get directions |
| Shop owner (vendor) | Get listed for free, receive bookings |
| Admin (GoLocal Wheels team) | Verify shops by phone, manage bookings and shops |
| Later: handloom/handcraft businesses | Location-based ads to tourists |

## Features – version 1 (done)
- Live map of shops with lowest price per day; filters (type, price, open now); sort; near me; directions
- Booking request saved with booking ID; “My bookings” status
- Shop owners can request a listing
- Admin: bookings with status, WhatsApp shortcuts, shops and bikes, call notes
- A shop is shown only after a logged phone call and approval
- Email alerts to admin

## Next (see SRS for IDs and priorities)
- Shop owner login (vendors update their own availability)
- OTP login for tourists, online payment (Razorpay), driving-licence check
- Reviews, refund tracker, SOS button, photo check-in/check-out
- Advertisement module for handloom/handcraft businesses

## Out of scope for version 1
Car rentals, inter-state one-way trips, insurance sales, a mobile app.

## Measurable targets
- 20 verified shops in Pondicherry before public launch
- Booking in under 3 minutes; pages load in under 3 seconds on 4G

## Deadline
Pondicherry public launch after 20 shops are verified (date to be agreed).
